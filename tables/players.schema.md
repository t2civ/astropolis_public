# Schema for players.tsv

Players are described in `players.descriptive.md`.


## Fields

- name (1st column) — Constructed per table instructions.
- player_class — One of POLITY, AGENCY, or COMPANY.
- start — Always TRUE (by default).
- part_of — If present, specifies that this player's population and activity are counted as part of another player.
- polity — National polity of the player.
- homeworld — Always Earth (by default).
- corporate_tax_rate — Game-start value: for a polity, its corporate tax, a share of the operating income of the private share of each of its facilities and its agencies' and companies' (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "Taxes"). Default 0.2.
- payroll_tax_rate — Game-start value: for a polity, its payroll tax, the share of every wage paid by its facilities and its agencies' and companies' that is withheld for its government treasury, from 0 to below 1. Seeded from recall at each polity's effective tax rate on labor in 2015, its taxes on labor income and its social contributions over what labor earned. Default 0.
- wealth_tax_rate — Game-start value: for a polity, its wealth tax, a share a year of what its residents hold past `wealth_tax_threshold`, which its AI moves (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "A government's taxes and transfers"). Default 0: in 2015 no polity here raised more than about a tenth of a percent of its output by a tax on net wealth.
- wealth_tax_threshold — Game-start value: for a polity, the wealth past which its wealth tax falls, a share of its residents' mean wealth; 0 taxes all of it alike. Default 1.
- reverse_tax_rate — Game-start value: for a polity, its reverse tax, a share a year of what each decile of its residents holds short of `reverse_tax_threshold`, paid to it out of the transfers its government treasury passes on, which its AI moves (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "A government's taxes and transfers"). Default 0: none in 2015 paid one on wealth.
- reverse_tax_threshold — Game-start value: for a polity, the wealth short of which its reverse tax pays, a share of its residents' mean wealth. Default 0.
- interest_tax_rate, dividend_tax_rate — Game-start values: for a polity, its income tax on interest and on dividends, the share of each paid to its residents that is withheld for its government treasury, from 0 to below 1 (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "Taxes"). Seeded from recall at each polity's rate on what its households held in taxable accounts in 2015, about halved for what pensions and tax-free accounts hold: nothing on deposit interest where it was exempt (China, Russia), and none on dividends at India, whose companies paid the tax on them. Default 0.
- tariff_rate — Game-start value: for a polity, its tariff on what its players take from another polity's, a share of the price the cash leg pays, seeding its rate on every resource traded at a locus's market, which its AI may move resource by resource (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "Taxes"; `TRADE_MODEL.md`, "The costs of movement"). Seeded from recall at each polity's effective rate in 2015, the customs duties it collected over what it imported, the EU's on what came from outside it (`EARTH_CALIBRATION.md`, "Public sector and tax"). Default 0.
- immigration_openness — Game-start value: for a polity, the share of an open border's flow of movers it lets in, from 0, closed, to 1 (`POPULATION_MODEL.md`, "Migration"). Where its places take people, they take them in proportion to it, and take part in the pool's prospect weighed by it. Seeded from recall of each polity's immigration policy in 2015; its government's to move ("A government's border"). Default 0.
- emigration_openness — Game-start value: for a polity, the share of an open border's flow of movers it lets out, from 0, closed, to 1. Where its places send people, they send them in proportion to it, and take part in the pool's prospect weighed by it. Default 1, a border its people are free to leave by.
- immigration_most — Game-start value: for a polity, its government's levels plan, the most of its free people a year it takes in while all the hours they offer are worked, its intake falling with the share worked (`POPULATION_MODEL.md`, "A government's border"). Its law at the game's start, which its government applies and a strategy may change. Seeded from recall of each polity's immigration policy in 2015. Blank for a player that is not a polity; a polity the tables don't seed plans at its AI's default.
- appropriation_share — Game-start value: for an agency, the most its polity's government treasury appropriates it, a share of that treasury's budget, which the base AI seeds as that budget's line (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "Budgets"). The agency's whole budget, which its facilities' funding lines divide. Seeded at each agency's 2015 budget over what its polity's government treasury took in a year over 2015's quarters in a game. Blank for players that are not agencies.
- provision_share — Game-start value: for a polity, what its government treasury spends on its residents' public provision, a share of that treasury's budget, which the base AI seeds as that budget's line (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`, "The treasuries' budgets"). It buys the public share of each want (`wants.tsv`, `public_share`) for every class alike as far as it goes. Seeded from recall at what each polity's governments spent in 2015 on health, education, public order and general public services less interest, over their revenue. Blank for players that are not polities, and for a polity that provides none.
